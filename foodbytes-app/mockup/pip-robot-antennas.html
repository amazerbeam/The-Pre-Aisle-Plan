Pip robot: antennas instead of hats

Cyberpunk only. The robot's five hat items stop sitting on the monitor head and replace the antenna instead. Most are a different antenna shape (cartoon and game robots: propeller caps, TV rabbit ears, boing springs, bug feelers, neon tubes). Only the chef hat leans on a charm: the red bulb becomes a tiny toque. Each item has two options; pick one per row and the lineup at the bottom updates. Item ids, slots and unlocks don't change. Herb and dragon keep their hats.

How it would build

  • Hat replaces the crown on the robot. A new REPLACES_CROWN flag on ROBOT_WARDROBE (like REPLACES_FACE for Smiley.exe). When it's set, PipFigure skips art.Crown and the hat draws from f.growTop with the form's growthRise, instead of from the hat anchor.
  • Each antenna takes (base, rise, droop), not (x, y, w), so it grows with the form and wilts like today's antenna (bent over, tip light off, animation stopped). The drooped state here is a generic tilt; the twin antennas (rabbit ears, feelers) splay instead.
  • Head bar and card crops read the antenna's top, the same way they read a hat's top today. The Dress Pip card thumbnails shown here use the existing hat crop unchanged, and every option fits.
  • Names change in WARDROBE_ITEM_LABELS for the robot only. Decided 2026-11-16: on the robot the Hat slot is labelled Antenna in Dress Pip (tab, card and slot heading); the slot key stays HAT and herb and dragon keep Hat.
  • New effects (propeller, sweep, wiggle, dangle, boing, whisk, tip blink, orbit) go in Pip.css and stop under Low FX and reduced motion like the others.
