Day kcal on recipe-card day buttons — proposal (FR-050 back)

Each day button shows that day's planned kcal (per person, all meals) above the day name, as it did before the redesign. Hover a day (or Tab onto it) to preview what the day becomes with this recipe in the slot; if another breakfast is there, the preview swaps it out. Click to add / replace / remove, exactly as today.

Same card and the same week in all three themes. Switch the variant to see the preview change.

  • Number: whole kcal, no comma (fits 7 buttons at 320px). Empty day shows a faint —.
  • States kept: planned here (brand / lime / wax fill + ✓), slot held by another recipe (dot / hazard + orange pip / dashed), free.
  • Preview: hover shows → new total in the theme accent (purple / glowing yellow / wax red). On a ✓ day the preview is the total without this recipe (what removing it leaves).
  • Touch: no preview; the number updates straight after the tap.
  • Size: the face grows from 40px to 48px tall (hit area 52px) to fit two lines.
  • Colours stay as theme tokens; no new literal colours in component CSS.
