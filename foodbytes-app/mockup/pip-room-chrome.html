Pip room chrome: role-token workbench

Every room UI surface (Shop with its four tabs, decor picker, Fridge, Trophy cabinet, Dress Pip, edit bar, head bar, check-in actions) in Default, Parchment and Cyberpunk, at 375 px and 1280 px, drawn with the live class names and the live room CSS. In this file every colour in that CSS reads a --pet-* role token (or an existing global token); the three .frame[data-theme] blocks set the proposed values. Contract 2 ports the table below.

What is a placeholder. The room art behind the sheets is a flat band: the room itself lives in pip-room-dragon.html and pip-room-robot.html. Shop and picker thumbnails and the Pip crops in Dress Pip are placeholder glyphs for the same reason. Station and decor names are the live herb names in Default, the lair names in Parchment and the workshop names in Cyberpunk, copied from the two room files' labels blocks.

Room art classification (up for review). The Fridge scene and the Trophy cabinet scene are classified as room art (Task 13): their surrounding sheet, head, buttons and text follow the tokens, while their painted interiors (the blue fridge, the wood cabinet, the gold awards) keep literal colours in every theme. If you would rather they follow the theme, say so at review and they move to tokens. No bespoke chrome looks are drawn (no parchment scroll sheets, no CRT panels): chrome is tokens only.
