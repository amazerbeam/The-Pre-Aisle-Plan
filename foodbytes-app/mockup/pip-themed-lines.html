Pip themed lines

One Pip, one points total, three skins. Default → basil (unchanged), Parchment → dragon (Hatchling → Wyrmling → Drake) and Cyberpunk → robot (Bot-kid → Teen Unit → Android). Each form comes from peak points, so no line ever shrinks. The bar shows current points and can drain. Every form is upright with two eyes at the eyes anchor, so all 7 wardrobe slots fit all 11 forms. Pip keeps its own palette in every theme.

Each creature wears its own version of every item. An item keeps its id, slot and unlock, but each line draws and names it its own way. Round specs are specs on basil, a battle scar on the dragon, and a Smiley.exe emoji face on the robot's screen. Several robot and dragon items are animated, and every animation stops under reduced motion and Low FX. Section 3 shows all 60 versions.

4 · Geometry and fit invariants (what wardrobe.check.mjs will assert)

  
The fit rules for every form of every line are crownTop ≤ hat.y, growTop ≤ hat.y, hat < eyes < neck < waist < feet, eyes under the brim (2·gap < hat.w), eyes inside the head (gap + 8·s < side), feet on the floor line (185–186), and forms never get smaller. The head bar must clear the crown, the head growth and every hat on each form that has a bar.
