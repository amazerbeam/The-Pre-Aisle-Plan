The rules

  1. Hats sit on the head anchor and scale to the hat width. Whatever grows from his head (sprout, leaves, flower) pokes out through the top of the hat, so growth is never hidden.
  2. Jackets, pants, belts and scarves are bands between the neck and waist lines, clipped to the body outline. A belt on the tiny seed and on Basilisk is the same item: the outline does the fitting.
  3. Glasses sit on the two eye points and scale with the face.
  4. Shoes sit on the two foot tips.
  5. Layer order, back to front: arms and crown → feet → shoes → body → pants → jacket → belt → scarf → outline → face → glasses → hat → head growth.

Limits to know about

  • No sleeves: the seed and seedling have no arms, so jackets are cut like vests. Basilisk's vines and the sprig's leaf arms stay bare.
  • Pants are worn on the lower body (Pip has no legs to fill), so they read as dungarees or shorts on the seed.
  • The seed is small: a full outfit is busy at footer-tab size. The tab could show the hat only.
  • Moods and wilting work on top of any outfit; the faces and leaves still change.
