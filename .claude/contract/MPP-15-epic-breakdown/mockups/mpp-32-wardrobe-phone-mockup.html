How it works

  1. Opening it: two buttons under the room, Wardrobe and Shop, in the strip that used to hold the hint text. The room art doesn't change. They use the same pill style as the check-in buttons in Pip's bubble (.pip-checkin-btn): small, but still 44px tall for a thumb. The Shop button is a placeholder for MPP-26, so here it only makes Pip say so.
  2. New unlocks: Pip announces them in his bubble (AC 6) and the Wardrobe button gets a red ! badge. The badge stays until you open the slot holding the new item.
  3. Phone layout: the dressing screen slides up and covers the room, the same way the fridge and cabinet panels do. ✕ closes it and returns focus to the Wardrobe button.
  4. No Save button: each tap puts the item on Pip and saves the outfit straight away. Tapping the worn item again takes it off.
  5. Slot tabs scroll sideways. A green dot means something is worn in that slot; a red dot means something new is waiting there.
  6. Each item card zooms in on the part of Pip it changes, drawn on his current form. You see how it will look on him, not a generic icon.
  7. Locked items are greyed out with a 🔒. Phones have no hover, so tapping one shows its unlock condition.
  8. Same outfit, every evolution starts collapsed so the item grid gets the space. Opening it shows all five forms; forms not reached yet are silhouettes.
  9. Footer tab: it shows Pip wearing his hat only (AC 5). A full outfit is too busy at that size.
