Pip as a sprout that evolves

Three evolution lines, each with five distinct forms, one per glow-up stage, like Bulbasaur → Ivysaur → Venusaur rather than one plant slowly getting bigger. Every stage-up is an evolution. Use Good week / Slip week to move the 30-day score and watch an evolution play, and try both slip rules.

Basilisk (herb stage 5) in every mood

Content and happy get the new confident face. Bored, mischievous and asleep keep the shared faces so the mood sheet stays consistent; when drooped (bored, asleep or wilting) the vines hang and the flower closes away.

How it would work

The score bands are placeholders. The real ones would come from the glow-up points.

On a slip: your call

  • Wilt (recommended): evolutions are permanent, as in Pokémon. A slip makes Pip wilt instead: leaves droop and colours fade, and a good week perks it back up. Evolving stays a real milestone.
  • De-evolve: the form follows the score both ways, like the current glow-up plan. This is more honest about the last 30 days, but losing an evolution feels harsh and is unlike any Pokémon.

What changes elsewhere

  • Replaces the body-stage glow-up shapes (belt, medal, muscles) from the progression mockup.
  • Wardrobe: five body shapes means a full outfit would need five fits (5× the art). Suggested: wardrobe items become accessories (hat, scarf, glasses, apron badge) that each form places at its own head/neck anchor points, so each item is drawn once.
  • Room, cooker walk and moods are unchanged: every form has feet.
  • The name stays the user's own. Form names (e.g. "Seedling") appear only as a caption.

Which line?

Tomato is my pick. Its colour journey (cream seed → green → orange → red) makes all five forms tell-apart-able even at the 24px footer-tab size, and it's food. Herb is the safest and most Pikmin-like. Pumpkin has the most dramatic final form but reads as Halloween.
