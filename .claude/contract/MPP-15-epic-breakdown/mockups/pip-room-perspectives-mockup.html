Pip's room — perspective options

Three ways to draw a two-wall room. Same furniture in each: bed, fridge, cooking area and the trophy cabinet (starts as planks on bricks). Use the level switch to see the upgrade path, and tap the cabinet to preview the full-screen trophy view (Phase D). Dashed boxes are empty decor spots.

What each option means

A · Corner, angled — most "room-like"; two real walls, so two sets of wall spots. Furniture against a wall is slanted to sit flat on it. Hardest to draw new items for (each wall item needs a slanted version).
B · Front-on with a side wall — closest to Focus Friend's look; one big back wall holds most items, a thin side wall gives the corner. Items are drawn straight-on, so new furniture is quick to add.
C · Flat side-on — today's room, just with furniture. Simplest, but it's really one wall.
