How it would work

Stage = points over the last 30 days. Each day earns up to 3: followed the plan, no snacks, no alcohol (0–90). 0–14 Mochi, 15–35 Warming up, 36–59 Toned, 60–89 Fit, 90 = Ripped (every day perfect for a month). It's a rolling window, so Pip can go backwards: every missed day eventually drops him a little, but gently, never overnight, and a few good days win it back. All thresholds are tunable.

Clothes are kept forever once unlocked, even if Pip loses shape.

Clothes are tied to specific achievements (shown under each item). Unlocked items stay in Pip's wardrobe forever; you choose what he wears.

The face, colour and kawaii style stay the same at every stage; only the shape, arms and muscle lines change.
