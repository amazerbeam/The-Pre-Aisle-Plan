Pip's room — v5 (check-in in Pip's bubble)

Interactive preview. Change the time of day to see Pip move, step through the upgrade levels, log a meal to watch Pip cook, answer the snack / alcohol check-in, and tap the fridge or the cabinet to look inside. Nothing here is saved.

Time of day

Morning: Pip hangs out in the middle. Meal time (around your MPP-13 meal times): Pip is at the cooker. Night (a few hours after your dinner time): Pip's asleep in bed.

Fridge & cooking

Meals planned this week:

Logging a meal sends Pip to the cooker to make it, and uses up one meal of food. Ticking off the shopping list fills the fridge back up. Tap the fridge to look inside; the gauge on top shows how full it is.

Upgrade level (bed, fridge, cooker, cabinet)

In the real thing each station levels up on its own; they move together here so you can see the whole path.

Check-in & streaks

Pip asks in his speech bubble: snacks first, then alcohol. Asked once a day; "Not now" skips and he asks again tomorrow, covering every day since your last answer. "Yes" asks when (defaults to today) and the streak restarts from that date. Ladder for plan, snacks and alcohol: 1, 3, 5, 7 days, 2 weeks, 3 weeks, 1 month, then it starts again; the cabinet counts how many times you've won each award.
