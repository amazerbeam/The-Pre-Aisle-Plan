Pip: new character options

Five directions, each drawn in every mood (content / happy / bored / mischievous / asleep) and through five growth stages that can go up and down with your last 30 days, as in the glow-up plan. Use the switches to compare; click a stage thumbnail to enlarge it. These are rough sketches to pick a direction from, not final art.

What the research says works

Finch: growth is the hook

The bird starts as a baby and visibly grows up as you check in. People praise the art, liken it to a Tamagotchi, and stay for the "it grew because of me" feeling.

Forest: progress you can lose

A tree grows while you focus and withers if you quit. Visible loss carries more weight than a number going down.

Pikmin: 3 readable stages

Leaf → bud → flower. Each stage is a single silhouette change you can read at thumbnail size.

BitePal / LittleMoochi: food pets

Each logged meal is a feeding, and skipping drains energy. LittleMoochi says "we don't label food as good or bad", so the pet reflects habits, not judgement.

Mascot design: silhouette first

Fill it black: can you still tell what it is? Round shapes read friendly. Keep the body, proportions and one signature accessory fixed, and let only the pose and face change (KEEPLI case study).

Why the current Pip feels flat

It's a plain lavender oval: the silhouette says "blob" with no story and no food link, and the body never changes, so there's nothing to grow into. Every option below fixes at least one of these.

My recommendation

A · Bao, with B · Sprout as runner-up.

Bao is the only option that is food, fully mobile and round-and-friendly at once. Its growth story fits a cooking app: a dough ball proves, gets steamed, then turns golden. The "slip" state is easy to read (it deflates a little) without feeling cruel. It also keeps everything already built or planned: the room, walking to the cooker, the wardrobe and the 5-stage glow-up.

Sprout shows progress best (Pikmin's leaf → bud → flower is proven), but it's a garden character in a kitchen app. Starter is the most charming idea and fits homemade-first, but a jar can't walk to the cooker or wear clothes, so MPP-17/18 and the wardrobe would need redesigning. The red panda is the most "pet-like" (Finch's model) but the art cost is highest and the food link only comes through its outfits.

The name can stay "Pip" whichever you choose, since it's user-editable anyway.
