Try it

  • All five meal tabs fit: no hidden Snacks / Extras off the right edge.
  • The week strip shows which days already have breakfast. An amber dot means the slot is empty. Tap an empty day to start picking for it: every card swaps its 7 day buttons for one big "Add to Sat" button.
  • Light / Moderate / Balanced is one tap, and the kcal and macros update with it.
  • ⋯ → Hide from my recipes hides a recipe and offers Undo. The Hidden (n) chip lists hidden recipes so you can bring them back.
  • A card is about half the height of today's, so you see roughly two and a half per screen instead of one and a bit.

Recipes, macros and the week's plan are illustrative, not read from the database.

Recipes: pick a meal for a gap, not hunt through 7 numbers

You come to the Recipes screen to fill your week. Today every card repeats the same seven daily calorie totals, shows the calories twice (as a dropdown and as a label), and shows no macros. Nothing tells you which days still need a breakfast. These ideas make the screen answer "what should I put where?". They also add the one thing you asked for: hiding recipes you never want to see.

The ideas

Spec panel: the rules this design encodes (please red-line)

  • Hide is per person (your partner on a shared plan keeps seeing it) and stored on the account, so it follows you across devices. Needs a small table, e.g. user_hidden_recipes(user_id, recipe_family_id or recipe_id), plus 3 endpoints. Hiding a recipe never removes it from your meal plan; it only leaves the Recipes list. Hiding hides the whole family (Light/Moderate/Balanced), not one variant.
  • Admins' existing "not live" recipes are a different thing (hidden for everyone). They keep working as today, and the menu wording is "Hide from my recipes" so the two don't get confused.
  • Day buttons have three states: empty (white), has another recipe (grey + dot, tooltip "Replaces Hash Browns…"), this recipe (filled ✓). They no longer show daily kcal; the week strip owns "what's in each day".
  • Picking mode (tap an empty day in the strip, or arrive from Meal Plan's "+ Add lunch" with ?mealType=lunch&date=2026-11-15): each card shows one "Add to Sun" button. Adding exits picking mode and returns to normal (or back to Meal Plan when you came from there).
  • Macro chips per serving use the meal traffic light (MACRO_TARGETS, already used by MacroBadgeRow in the recipe view). kcal is shown, never judged. Needs P/C/F per serving on the recipe summary (the card's list endpoint doesn't carry them today).
  • Variant segment only appears when the family has ≥2 members. Default selection follows today's rule: the variant already in your plan, otherwise Moderate.
  • Servings chip opens the same stepper (0.25–20). It starts at your default portions (MPP-3).
  • Cheat badge stays for everyone; Audited stays admin-only. The coloured stripe is replaced by a purple left edge that means "this recipe is in your plan this week".
