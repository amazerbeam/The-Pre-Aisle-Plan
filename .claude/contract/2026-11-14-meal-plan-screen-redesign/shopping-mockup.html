Try it

  • Tap an item to tick it. It drops to the bottom of its aisle (as today) and the progress bar fills.
  • Tick everything in an aisle and the aisle folds into a green "✓ done" row and moves to the end, so the next aisle you need is always at the top.
  • ⓘ shows which meals need that ingredient. Today that's a hidden 1-second long-press, which still works.
  • Hide ticked shortens the list while you're in the shop.
  • 🔆 Keep screen on stops the phone sleeping mid-aisle (the same wake lock the recipe view already uses).
  • ⋯ holds Regenerate, Uncheck all and Change dates. The new-list sheet suggests this week in one tap.

Items, quantities and the breakdown are illustrative, not read from the database.

Shopping: keep what works, make it fit the new look

The shopping list already does the hard part well: one persisted list, grouped by aisle, ticks that sync, checked items sinking to the bottom. These ideas don't rebuild it. They bring it into the new header/card style and fix the few things that get in the way while you're standing in the shop.

The ideas

Spec panel (please red-line)

  • No behaviour changes to ticking, sorting, persistence or generation. Same ShoppingListContext calls (toggleItem, uncheckAll, generateShoppingList).
  • Done aisles are a display rule only: an aisle with every item ticked collapses and renders after the unfinished aisles. Tapping it re-opens it. Unticking anything brings it back in place.
  • Hide ticked is a per-device view toggle (localStorage, try/catch), shown with "12 ticked hidden". It never unticks anything.
  • ⓘ calls the same breakdown endpoint as the long-press (FR-042); long-press keeps working. It's hidden on ticked rows (as the long-press is today).
  • New-list default range = the week shown in the header pill (MealPlanContext start/end). "Choose dates" reveals today's two date inputs. The "replaces your current list" warning stays.
  • Out-of-date banner is driven by a new server-side boolean on the shopping-list response (planChangedSinceGenerated): the plan owner's entries or extra choices in the list's range changed after the list's generated_at. A removal leaves no row behind, so that case must be detectable too (e.g. record a "plan changed at" time per owner on every add/remove/move/choice change). That's decided at planning. Regenerate keeps the list's own dates. The unused shoppingListInvalidated localStorage flag is removed.
  • Keep screen on uses the existing useWakeLock + WakeLockIcon. It's off by default and released when you leave the page.
  • Desktop shows aisles in two columns; phones show one, with the progress bar sticky under the header.
