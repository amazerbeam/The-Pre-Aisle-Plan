Recipe view: built for cooking, not just reading

The recipe view is where you actually use the app with flour on your hands. Today it's a single narrow column: a purple header, then a long ingredient table, then the steps far below. Variants hide inside a calorie dropdown, and the "keep screen on" control is a padlock emoji. These ideas keep every feature and make the screen work at the stove.

The ideas

Spec panel (please red-line)

  • Nothing new is stored. Ingredient ticks, step progress and cook mode are screen-only and reset when the view closes. The variant, servings and eaten tick behave exactly as today.
  • Macros show P g · C % · F % per serving with the same colours as the cards and plan (MACRO_TARGETS). Tapping a chip still opens MacroTargetPopup.
  • Fractions: quantities round to the nearest ¼ / ⅓ / ½ / ¾ for counts, tbsp, tsp and cups (0.50 → ½, 1.25 → 1¼, 0.33 → ⅓). Grams and ml stay whole numbers. It's display only; the scaling maths is unchanged.
  • Keep screen on is the existing useWakeLock, still on by default, now shown as a labelled toggle in the header. It's hidden where the browser doesn't support it.
  • Extras: the Homemade / Store-bought toggle edits the same choice the add-to-day popup sets (HomemadeSelectionsContext, or the meal's own choices when opened from the plan, as with MPP-10). Store-bought swaps the linked step to its alt_instruction, as today.
  • Cook mode shows one step at a time, with the ingredients that step names (matched by name in the step text). Wake lock is forced on while it's open.
  • Plan context bar appears only when opened from a meal plan entry: "Sat · Dinner · 👥 2" plus the existing eaten button (one tap; Undo keeps working, MPP-12). The bottom eaten section stays as well.
  • Layout: desktop is a wide modal (up to 1040px) with ingredients in a sticky left column and steps on the right; mobile is full screen with Ingredients | Steps tabs and a header that stays put.
