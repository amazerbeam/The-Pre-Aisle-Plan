# Soldering skill ladder

> **Scope.** Structural: the rungs, tools, technique, safety. Live: specific product prices and models — search before recommending a buy.

The user starts at **rung 0** (never soldered, as of 2026-11-14). Advance a rung only when the user reports completing its exit task. When unsure where they are, ask once, then remember it within the session.

| Rung | Can do | Exit task to advance |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Nothing yet | Buy tools + a through-hole practice kit |
| 1 | Screwdriver work: teardown, cleaning, Edge stick modules, membranes | Strip and reassemble one controller with no broken clips/ribbons |
| 2 | Connectors: ribbon cables, battery swaps | Replace a ribbon or battery successfully |
| 3 | Through-hole soldering: practice kit built, clean shiny joints | Finish the practice kit; all joints pass inspection |
| 4 | Through-hole **desoldering**: multi-pin parts, stick modules | Remove and refit a multi-pin part from a scrap board without lifting pads; then do the iFixit "cut the module apart" stick method |
| 5 | Fine SMD: USB-C port, small components | Several stick swaps done cleanly; practise SMD on scrap first (hot air helpful) |
| 6 | Board-level diagnosis (charging circuits, shorts) | Out of scope — recommend parts-only |

## Starter tools
Temperature-controlled iron (e.g. Pinecil-class), chisel + fine tips; 63/37 leaded solder (easier for beginners) or lead-free if preferred; **flux** (liberal use is what makes desoldering work); desoldering pump + wick; isopropyl alcohol + swabs; tweezers; opening picks + spudger; #00 Phillips; Kapton tape; helping hands. Later: hot air station (big help for stick removal), multimeter.

## Technique notes worth repeating
- Tin the tip; clean it on brass wool; heat the pad and pin, feed solder to the joint, not the tip.
- ~330–370 °C for through-hole; add fresh leaded solder to stubborn lead-free joints to lower the melt point before sucking it out.
- Don't hold heat on a pad for long — that's how pads lift.
- Don't move the joint while it solidifies.

## Safety
Ventilate (fume extractor or open window + fan), wash hands after leaded solder, eye protection when clipping leads, iron in a stand, disconnect the battery before any soldering on a controller, never puncture or heat the Li-ion cell.
